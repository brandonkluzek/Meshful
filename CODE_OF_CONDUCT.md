# Code of conduct

Meshful welcomes thoughtful participation from learners, educators, engineers,
and researchers. Treat other participants with respect, assume good faith while
remaining specific about evidence, and make room for people with different
backgrounds and levels of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and observed behavior rather than attacking people.
- Give reproducible, constructive feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and the boundaries of learner data.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, sexualized conduct, threats, doxxing, and
  deliberate disruption.
- Follow maintainer directions when a discussion needs to pause, narrow, or move
  to a safer reporting channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these
expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The
response should be proportionate to the behavior, its impact, and whether it is
repeated.

Report conduct concerns through a private contact route listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/brandonkluzek). Do not include
personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Concerns involving GitHub-hosted
content may also be reported through
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

This policy applies in the repository and in project spaces where someone is
representing Meshful.
