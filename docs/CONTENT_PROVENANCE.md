# Public example content provenance

The repository ships three small examples totaling 18 cards. They demonstrate
product behavior; they are not a complete curriculum, an independently
reviewed benchmark, or evidence of learning outcomes.

The card wording in `web/data/catalog.js` was freshly authored for this
release on 2026-09-02 under owner direction. It summarizes general technical
facts in original language. Each exact card record retains one or more public
reference URLs in `sourceRefs`. No private course payload or private source
material was used in the public example catalog.

| Example | Cards | Reference basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Linear Algebra I | 4 | MIT OpenCourseWare 18.06 calendar and lecture transcripts |
| Introductory Mechanics | 12 | OpenStax University Physics Volume 1 sections on kinematics, force, work, energy, and momentum |
| Software Engineering Foundations | 2 | Gradle, Microsoft Learn, and Python standard-library documentation |

The references support factual alignment and provenance. They do not make the
examples independently reviewed. The exact owner-controlled example text is
dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal through `LICENSES/README.md`, so reuse does
not require attribution. The source links are provenance records, not
attribution conditions, and no linked third-party material is relicensed.

## Academic Deck Library

The repository also ships the sanitized `2026-09-02.public-sanitized-72.v2`
academic Deck Library release: 72 courses, 9,988 cards, 17,712 required prerequisite links,
and 770 cross-course links. It was AI-authored, received full-card AI review
and second-reader repairs, and is explicitly not certified by human
subject-matter experts. Public-source metadata and card payloads have empty
source locator lists. Private construction evidence and source locators are
not distributed.

The exact 75 payload files are pinned by `library/MANIFEST.json` and released
under CC BY 4.0 through `library/CONTENT-LICENSE-NOTICE.md`.

Future decks may be added only through a new exact-byte release with a recorded
rights decision, deterministic validation, and an explicit review label.
